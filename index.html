About Tern's Exim

India's Trusted Merchant Export Company

Tern's Exim is a merchant export company based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu — our primary business is the export of engineered products to global markets. We specialize in industrial fasteners and solar panel mounting systems, supplying international buyers with strict quality control and reliable logistics via Kochi Port.

Our fasteners conform to internationally recognized standards including DIN 931, DIN 933, DIN 934, ISO 3506, ISO 898-1, IEC 62548 and BIS — making import clearance smooth for buyers across Europe, USA and the UAE.

From SS304 and SS316 stainless steel bolts and nuts to complete solar rooftop mounting kits, every product is export-packaged with full documentation — material test reports, certificates of conformity and commercial invoices included.
